# Perspective Crime — creative design direction

## Ambition

Build an original short 3D geometric-illusion puzzle with the sense of wonder, crafted composition, and polished player experience associated with *Monument Valley*. The work must have its own geometry, level design, interactions, visual identity and assets.

We are **not prescribing the content of the level**. The developer should invent a memorable spatial idea and make that idea playable and delightful. It may use perspective coincidence, impossible geometry, topology changes, movable architecture, projections, hidden paths, camera alignment or a combination. Choose what serves the experience, not what fits the initial demo.

A single beautifully finished puzzle is a complete and worthwhile deliverable. Polish it thoroughly instead of accumulating many crude scenes.

## Creative and art production

Treat the visual direction as part of design, not postprocessing. Develop a coherent composition, palette, lighting, scene rhythm, interactive visual feedback, and deliberate transitions. Actively use available image-generation models to create original visual material where it benefits the work, then curate and integrate it with the game's geometry. Create original visual assets rather than copying recognizable levels, graphics or characters from reference games.

The player's experience is the specification: discover the rule, experiment with spatial relationships, see a surprising yet legible consequence, reach a meaningful ending, and want to show it to somebody else.

## Engineering and delivery

Use the existing Godot 4 repository as a starting point, not a cage. You can replace the starter platforms, camera and projection-activated bridge completely. Pursue a reliable, smooth, offline, publicly presentable playable game. Provide a playable export/build, visual demonstration, and accurate testing notes.

Automated tests should validate mechanisms, but cannot judge game feel, visual design or intuitiveness. The final milestone requires genuine graphical playtesting.

See [the revised delivery brief](PR1_SPEC.md) and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). Any old “The Alibi” graybox details are historical prototype notes, not mandatory requirements.
